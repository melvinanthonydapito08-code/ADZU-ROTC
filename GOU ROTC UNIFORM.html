ROTC Uniform Guidelines

General Office Uniform (GOU) Guidelines

DOs ✔

  • Maintain Proper Dress and Bearing - Ensure all placements of Rank, Pins, Nametag, and other accessories are correct and comply with regulations.


  • Keep it Impeccably Clean - The uniform must be clean, pressed, and stain-free. Footwear must be highly polished ("shined").


  • Wear for Official Functions Only - Wear the uniform strictly for official ROTC duties, training, formations, and ceremonies.


  • Show Proper Respect - Render the correct military salute to superior officers.


  • Grooming Standards - Ensure your hair and overall appearance meet the established military grooming standards while in uniform.

DON'Ts ✖

  • Don't Wear for Personal Business - Do not use the uniform for unauthorized personal errands like shopping, watching movies, or casual public outings.


  • Don't Wear Incomplete - Never wear "half-uniforms" (e.g., uniform trousers with civilian attire). The uniform must be worn as a complete set.


  • Don't Put Hands in Pockets - Keep your hands out of your pockets, as this is considered unprofessional and undisciplined.


  • Don't Use Prohibited Items - Smoking, consuming alcohol, or gambling is prohibited while in uniform, especially in public.


  • Don't Carry Bulky Items - Avoid having noticeable bulk in your pockets (like large cell phones or wallets) that ruins the uniform's smooth appearance.


  • Don't Wear Unauthorized Accessories - Do not wear excessive or flashy jewelry; keep any necessary accessories simple and conservative.
